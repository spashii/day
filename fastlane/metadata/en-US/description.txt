Day shows the calendars Google lists for each of your accounts as one, read-only.

The next event is on your Lock Screen, above or below the clock. The month names its big events: multi-day events are bars, one-offs that matter get a title, and recurring events are dots.

- Several Google accounts in one view.
- A Lock Screen widget in three sizes.
- Join for Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex and Whereby links.
- Open in Google Calendar to edit anything.
- Colors are Google's. Light and dark follow your iPhone.

Day does not edit events, send replies or send notifications. Google Calendar does that, one tap away.

Day reads your calendars from Google and keeps them on your devices. Your sign-in moves between them in iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted. To show changes as they happen, Google tells Day's server at apps.idea2prod.in that a calendar changed, never what changed, and the server keeps only random IDs, push addresses and the device type.
