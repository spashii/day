Privacy

Day reads your calendars from Google and keeps them on this device. Day itself collects nothing. On the Mac, it also checks for updates through apps.idea2prod.in, which counts checks and downloads by app, version and country and never stores your IP address.

  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and no account of its own.
  • Your calendars go between your device and Google, and nowhere else.
  • Your events, guest names and email addresses, your calendar list and the calendars you chose to show are stored on your device. The widgets read the same saved events and the same Google sign-in, on the same device.
  • The sign-in Google gives Day is kept in your device's keychain.
  • Day asks Google for read-only access to your calendars and for your email address, which it shows beside each account. It cannot add, change or delete anything in Google Calendar.
  • Removing an account in Day deletes its saved events and its sign-in from your device. You can also end Day's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.
  • Open in Google Calendar and Join hand a link to Google Calendar, your browser or the call app. Their own policies apply from there.

Day's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
